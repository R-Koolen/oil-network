  • The three columns are not on one scale: production and consumption are in thousand barrels per day (kb/d), refining throughput is in thousand tonnes per year (kt/yr). Node height is scaled within its column only; very small values are drawn at a minimum height.
  • Line width is trade value in US dollars, one scale for both crude and product flows. Tonnes are not used: some tonnage figures in the trade data are unreliable.
  • Flows inside a country (for example, a country refining its own crude) are not in the data and are not drawn.
  • A country marked “no data” appears because a drawn flow starts or ends there, but the source has no number for it in that column.
  • Hover over a node or a line for its values.

Data sources
